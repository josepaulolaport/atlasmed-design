# Atlasmed Platform Overview

## Summary

Atlasmed is an AI-powered healthcare relationship and intelligence platform. It helps pharmaceutical and healthcare commercial teams manage healthcare relationships, territories, visits, tasks, data, and workflows while giving clinics and physicians controlled ways to interact with the ecosystem.

## Primary Users

- Pharmaceutical sales representatives.
- Sales managers and regional managers.
- Healthcare commercial operations teams.
- Clinics and medical practices.
- Physicians.
- Healthcare organizations and partners.
- Future enterprise healthtech customers.

## Core Capabilities

- Multi-tenant organization management.
- Healthcare CRM for clinics, physicians, contacts, visits, and relationships.
- Territory management and clinic assignment workflows.
- Task, follow-up, and calendar reminders.
- Dashboard and analytics for field activity, coverage, and performance.
- AI assistant for questions and actions across CRM, territory, analytics, and workflow data.
- Admin console for users, permissions, audit logs, integrations, and AI governance.

## Product Principles

- Healthcare data must be handled with privacy, auditability, and least-privilege access.
- Field workflows must be fast on mobile and useful offline where needed.
- Managers need trustworthy analytics and clear traceability from activity to outcomes.
- AI actions must be governed, observable, reversible where practical, and permission-aware.
- The system should start as a modular monolith and evolve by domain boundaries, not premature service decomposition.
